# Site Web

## Purpose and Design Decisions

The `helprest-site` package is the public web presence and regulatory compliance portal for HelpRest. It is built as a **Next.js 16 App Router** application configured with **Static Site Generation (`output: 'export'`, `trailingSlash: false`)**, emitting real pre-rendered `.html` files into `out/` so that direct browser navigation, Google Play Console crawlers, and regulatory bots receive genuine **HTTP 200 OK** HTML documents on every route without relying on SPA client rewrites.

It serves two interconnected primary functions:
1. **Product Discovery & Conversion**: Introduces HelpRest to consumers and restaurant administrators through a commercial product lens aligned with the mobile app (`helprest-app`). It demonstrates real-time dietary flag filtering (`RecommendationService` rules including zero-match exclusion), `"Para você" / "Outros Itens"` menu separation with ingredient inspection, and 100-meter GPS geofencing for visit reviews, showcasing the Android Google Play package (`com.helprest`) and announcing the forthcoming iOS App Store release.
2. **Google Play Store & LGPD Regulatory Compliance**: Houses the mandatory public legal and operational static pages required by Google Play Console policies and Brazilian data privacy law (**Lei nº 13.709/2018 — LGPD**):
   - **Privacy Policy (`/privacidade`, alias `/privacy`)**: Prominent Disclosure for optional background location (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`), camera usage (`expo-image-picker`), Google Sign-In OAuth2 (no traditional password/Argon2id storage), Google Maps Platform, CNPJ partner data, zero financial collection, zero third-party ad SDKs, `Art. 11, I` specific consent for sensitive health/dietary restrictions, `Art. 14` (`13+` minimum age, `13–17` parental assistance), `Art. 18, §5º` / `Art. 19, II` **15-day** elimination deadline, full elimination of textual reviews/photos upon deletion, `Art. 20` automated decision review, `Art. 33` / `Res. ANPD 19/2024` international transfers, `Art. 18, §1º` ANPD petition right, and cross-link to Child Safety Standards.
   - **Terms of Use (`/termos`, alias `/terms`)**: UGC moderation/reporting rules (`suporte@helprest.net` and in-app reporting), `100m` geofencing anti-fraud rules, `13+` / `Art. 14` age requirements, and **Child Safety Standards (CSAE / CSAM)** section at `/termos#seguranca-infantil`.
   - **Account and Data Deletion (`/exclusao-de-dados`, alias `/data-deletion`)**: Real pre-rendered static page offering Android device permission/JWT session revocation instructions alongside a public web protocol generator forwarding verified ownership requests to `privacidade@helprest.net` (immediate or max **15 calendar days** operational purge including individual visits, textual comments, photos, and coordinates; only non-singularizable aggregated establishment averages under `Art. 12` / `Art. 16, IV` or isolated statutory holds under `Art. 16, I`, plus `<= 30`-day cyclic backup overwrite).
   - **Data Safety Section breakdown (`/seguranca-dos-dados`, alias `/data-safety`)**: Mirrors Play Console declarations, Android permission matrix, HTTPS (`TLS 1.2/1.3`) + Google OAuth2 (JWKS via `jose` & HS256 JWT) security, SDK disclosures, non-financial and non-government status.
   - **Health and Medical Disclaimer (`/aviso-de-saude`, alias `/health-disclaimer`)**: Complies with Play Store Health Content policies, clarifies Food & Drink non-medical positioning (without Health Connect / clinical APIs), and highlights cross-contamination warnings.
   - **Developer Support & Contact Center (`/suporte`, aliases `/support`, `/contato`)**: Lists official developer/DPO details (`Gustavo L. Gregorio`), direct `@helprest.net` emails, `com.helprest` package identifier, and support form.

The package uses **Next.js 16 (App Router SSG)**, **React 19**, **TypeScript** (`strict: true`), **Tailwind CSS v4**, and bundled self-hosted variable fonts (**Bricolage Grotesque Variable**, **Plus Jakarta Sans Variable**, and **JetBrains Mono Variable** via `@fontsource-variable/*`). It pairs Next.js App Router files (`src/app/layout.tsx`, `providers.tsx`, `globals.css`, `not-found.tsx`, `sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`, and `src/app/**/page.tsx`) with **Atomic Design** views (`src/views/*`) and components (`atoms`, `molecules`, `organisms`, `templates`), keeping global styles and font imports in `src/app/layout.tsx` and client interactivity isolated behind explicit `'use client'` boundaries.

## Invariants

- **True Static HTML Pre-rendering (`output: 'export'`)**: Every canonical route (`/`, `/privacidade`, `/exclusao-de-dados`, `/seguranca-dos-dados`, `/aviso-de-saude`, `/termos`, `/suporte`) and documented alias (`/privacy`, `/data-deletion`, `/data-safety`, `/health-disclaimer`, `/terms`, `/support`, `/contato`) must be generated as a real static `.html` file inside `out/` during `npm run build`, serving **HTTP 200 OK** with complete semantic HTML before JS hydration.
- **Canonical Metadata & Discovery Files**: Every App Router `page.tsx` exports static `Metadata` (`title`, `description`, `alternates.canonical` pointing to the canonical PT-BR path on `https://helprest.net`). `src/app/sitemap.ts` (`force-static`) and `src/app/robots.ts` (`force-static`) generate `out/sitemap.xml` and `out/robots.txt`; `src/app/not-found.tsx` generates `out/404.html`.
- **Product-Centric Stance**: The portal describes HelpRest strictly as a commercial technology product. Academic, institutional, or university research artifacts (e.g., MEPI, scientific initiation) must never be referenced on public pages.
- **Neutral Canvas & Exact Platform Cyan-Teal Hierarchy**: Uses bright crisp neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7F7F8`, `#F1F2F4`, `#E6E8EB`) paired with primary ink `#11181C` (aligned with `helprest-app/constants/Colors.ts`), body ink `#3F454A`, muted ink `#596168`, and control borders `#717880` (`>= 3:1`), with `@media (forced-colors: active)` fallbacks. Primary accents use exact app cyan-teal `#009C9D` (`hover: #1BA6A7`, dark ink on fill `#09181A`, soft tint `#EFFBFB`, accessible inline link/small-text `#00797A` at `>= 4.5:1`), coral heart accent `#FF533F`, danger tokens `#B42318`/`#FDF1EF`/`#7A271A`, and rating stars `#E5A910` (gold omitted outside star ratings).
- **Factual Backend/Auth Alignment & LGPD Compliance**: Public copy must match actual system implementation (`helprest-api` uses Google Sign-In OAuth2 + Google JWKS + JWT, with zero password/Argon2id login) and LGPD rules (`15`-day elimination deadline under `Art. 18, §5º` / `Art. 19, II`; no post-deletion preservation of textual reviews or photos under `"desvinculação"`; explicit `Art. 11, I` sensitive health consent for dietary restrictions).
- **Honest Client-Side Flows & Accessible Semantics**: Forms and download dialogs must never simulate fake backend submission success when no server endpoint exists; they generate user-ready protocols/summaries backed by explicit `mailto:` dispatch (`suporte@helprest.net`, `privacidade@helprest.net`), never nest `<button>` inside `<a>`/`<Link>`, use interaction-gated validation (`.hr-field-control:is(:user-invalid, ...)`), CSS breadcrumb separators (`content: "/" / ""`), and native `<search>` / `<dialog>` / Popover API semantics.
- **Strict Linting & Typing**: All source files must compile without errors under `tsc --noEmit` (`strict: true`), pass Next ESLint (`eslint .`) without errors, and build cleanly with `next build`.

## Anti-Patterns

- **SPA Client-Only Routing for Compliance URLs**: Shipping a single `index.html` shell that returns HTTP 404 on `/exclusao-de-dados` or `/privacidade` to crawlers and Google Play reviewers.
- **LGPD Non-Compliant Retention Claims**: Claiming a 30-day response window (GDPR baseline instead of LGPD `15` days) or keeping user-authored reviews/photos published in `"desvinculado"` form after account deletion.
- **Inaccurate Security/Auth Claims**: Claiming password hashing (`Argon2id`) when authentication is strictly Google Sign-In OAuth2.
- **Academic Context Leakage**: Mentioning thesis, faculty, scientific initiation, or MEPI on user-facing web pages.
- **Interactive Nesting & AI Clutter**: Wrapping `<button>` inside `<Link>`/`<a>`, tinting canvases greenish (`#F4F6F5`), stacking nested gray boxes inside white legal containers, or wrapping inline monospace identifiers in white pills.
- **External Unreliable Font/Stock Dependencies**: Linking unverified third-party stock avatars/thumbnails or external Google Fonts CDNs when local bundled `@fontsource-variable` fonts and local showcase imagery are available.
