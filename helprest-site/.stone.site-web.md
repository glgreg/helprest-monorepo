# Site Web

## Purpose and Design Decisions

The `helprest-site` package is the web presence and landing portal for HelpRest. It serves two interconnected primary functions:
1. **Product Discovery & Conversion**: Introduces HelpRest to consumers and restaurant administrators through a commercial product lens aligned with the mobile app (`helprest-app`). It demonstrates real-time dietary flag filtering (`RecommendationService` rules including zero-match exclusion), `"Para você" / "Outros Itens"` menu separation with ingredient inspection, and 100-meter GPS geofencing for visit reviews, showcasing the Android Google Play package (`com.helprest`) and announcing the forthcoming iOS App Store release.
2. **Google Play Store & Regulatory Compliance**: Houses the mandatory public legal and operational pages required by Google Play Console policies and Brazilian data privacy regulations (LGPD):
   - Privacy Policy (`/privacidade`): includes Prominent Disclosure for optional background location (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`), camera usage, Google Sign-In OAuth2, Google Maps Platform, CNPJ partner data, zero financial collection, zero third-party ad SDKs, `13+` age threshold, and cross-link to Child Safety Standards
   - Terms of Use (`/termos`): includes UGC moderation/reporting rules (`suporte@helprest.net`), `100m` geofencing anti-fraud rules, `13+` age requirement, and **Child Safety Standards (CSAE / CSAM)** section at `/termos#seguranca-infantil`
   - Account and Data Deletion (`/exclusao-de-dados`): provides honest Android device permission/session revocation instructions alongside a public web protocol generator forwarding verified ownership requests to `privacidade@helprest.net`
   - Data Safety Section breakdown (`/seguranca-dos-dados`): mirrors Play Console declarations, Android permission matrix, TLS 1.3 + Argon2id security, SDK disclosures, non-financial and non-government status
   - Health and Medical Disclaimer (`/aviso-de-saude`): complies with Play Store Health Content policies, clarifies Food & Drink non-medical positioning (without Health Connect / clinical APIs), and highlights cross-contamination warnings
   - Developer Support and Contact Center (`/suporte`): lists official developer details (`Gustavo L. Gregorio`), direct `@helprest.net` emails, `com.helprest` package identifier, and support form

The package is built with **Vite 6**, **React 19**, **TypeScript** (strict mode), **Tailwind CSS v4**, and bundled self-hosted variable fonts (**Bricolage Grotesque Variable**, **Plus Jakarta Sans Variable**, and **JetBrains Mono Variable** via `@fontsource-variable/*`). It follows **Atomic Design** (`atoms`, `molecules`, `organisms`, `templates`, `pages`), keeping `main.tsx` as a pure root mount importing font/CSS assets without inline HTML elements.

`HomePage` renders a focused consumer and partner conversion sequence alternating clean neutral bands: `HeroSection` (featuring the 4-tab `PhoneMockup`: `Início`, `Lugares`, `Favoritos`, `Social`, plus interactive `details/[id]` view), `InteractiveSimulator`, `HowItWorksSection`, `ForEstablishmentsSection`, `ReviewsSection`, `DataSafetyHighlightSection`, `FaqSection`, and `DownloadCtaSection`.

## Invariants

- **Product-Centric Stance**: The portal describes HelpRest strictly as a commercial technology product. Academic, institutional, or university research artifacts (e.g., MEPI, scientific initiation) must never be referenced on public pages.
- **Neutral Canvas & Exact Platform Cyan-Teal Hierarchy**: Uses bright crisp neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7F7F8`, `#F1F2F4`, `#E6E8EB`) paired with primary ink `#11181C` (aligned with `helprest-app/constants/Colors.ts`), body ink `#3F454A`, muted ink `#596168`, and control borders `#717880` (`>= 3:1`), with `@media (forced-colors: active)` fallbacks. Primary accents use exact app cyan-teal `#009C9D` (`hover: #1BA6A7`, dark ink on fill `#09181A`, soft tint `#EFFBFB`, accessible inline link/small-text `#00797A` at `>= 4.5:1`), coral heart accent `#FF533F`, danger tokens `#B42318`/`#FDF1EF`/`#7A271A`, and rating stars `#E5A910` (gold omitted outside star ratings). Layered borderless tonal surfaces, editorial hairline dividers inside legal prose, and variable typography (`Bricolage Grotesque Variable` display, `Plus Jakarta Sans Variable` UI/body, `JetBrains Mono Variable` monospace) replace boxy cards and decorative icon clutter.
- **Honest Client-Side Flows**: Forms and download dialogs must never simulate fake backend submission success when no server endpoint exists; they generate clear user-ready protocols/summaries backed by explicit `mailto:` dispatch to official addresses (`suporte@helprest.net`, `privacidade@helprest.net`).
- **Atomic Separation of Concerns**: Pages are assembled solely from organisms and templates; organisms from molecules and atoms. The application entry point `main.tsx` renders `<App />` via `createRoot` without raw HTML markup.
- **Client Routing Integrity**: `RouterProvider` / `useRouter` handles client history, deep links, route+hash anchor scrolling (including `/#...` and `/termos#seguranca-infantil`), and automatic scroll-to-top on navigation.
- **Mandatory Play Store Compliance URLs**: The routes `/privacidade`, `/termos` (`#seguranca-infantil`), `/exclusao-de-dados`, `/seguranca-dos-dados`, `/aviso-de-saude`, and `/suporte` must remain accessible and functional, as they are referenced in the Google Play Console listing and compliance declarations.
- **Strict Linting & Typing**: All source files must compile without errors under `tsc -b` (`strict: true`) and pass `eslint .` without warnings or errors.

## Integration Contracts

- **App Package ID (Web Target Identifier)**: `com.helprest` (public Android package identifier referenced across `helprest-site` download flows, Play Console metadata, and legal/support pages; companion `helprest-app` rename happens separately).
- **Official Web Domain**: `https://helprest.net/`.
- **Official Developer Entity**: `Gustavo L. Gregorio` (Curitiba, PR — Brasil).
- **Official Support & Child Safety Email**: `suporte@helprest.net`.
- **DPO / Privacy Contact**: `privacidade@helprest.net`.
- **Dietary Flags Domain Alignment**: Flag IDs (`gluten_free`, `lactose_free`, `vegan`, `vegetarian`, `nut_free`, `seafood_free`, `egg_free`, `sugar_free`) and match scoring logic match `helprest-api` domain entities (`Flag.ts`, `RecommendationService.ts`) and `helprest-app` constants.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Academic Context Leakage**: Mentioning thesis, faculty, scientific initiation, or MEPI on user-facing web pages.
- **Raw HTML in `main.tsx`**: Placing headers, divs, or inline styling inside `main.tsx` instead of encapsulating them into Atomic components and templates.
- **Over-Green Canvas or Nested AI Card Clutter**: Tinting page backgrounds greenish (`#F4F6F5`), stacking nested gray boxes inside white legal containers, placing decorative icons ahead of numbered headings, or wrapping inline monospace identifiers in white pills.
- **Broken Legal Routes or Fake Compliance**: Merging policies into a single document, using modal-only terms, omitting CSAE/CSAM disclosures, or faking server form submissions without actually triggering a verified user channel.
- **External Unreliable Font/Stock Dependencies**: Linking unverified third-party stock avatars/thumbnails or external Google Fonts CDNs when local bundled `@fontsource-variable` fonts and watermark-free showcase imagery are available.
- **Bypassing ESLint or TypeScript**: Disabling strict mode or using `any` types in page or component props.
