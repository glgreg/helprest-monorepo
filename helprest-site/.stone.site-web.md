# Site Web

## Purpose and Design Decisions

The `helprest-site` package is the web presence and landing portal for HelpRest. It serves two interconnected primary functions:
1. **Product Discovery & Conversion**: Introduces HelpRest to consumers and restaurant administrators through a commercial product lens. It highlights the dietary flag recommendation algorithm, verified safe kitchens, and the 100-meter GPS geofencing requirement for visit reviews, driving downloads to the Google Play Store (`com.gustavo_lg.helprest`) and announcing the forthcoming iOS App Store release.
2. **Google Play Store & Regulatory Compliance**: Houses the mandatory public legal and operational pages required by Google Play Console policies and Brazilian data privacy regulations (LGPD):
   - Privacy Policy (`/privacidade`)
   - Terms of Use (`/termos`)
   - Account and Data Deletion (`/exclusao-de-dados`), offering both in-app steps and a web request workflow
   - Data Safety Section breakdown (`/seguranca-dos-dados`) mirroring Play Console declarations
   - Health and Medical Disclaimer (`/aviso-de-saude`) complying with Play Store Health Content policies
   - Developer Support and Contact Center (`/suporte`)

The package is built with **Vite 6**, **React 19**, **TypeScript** (strict mode), and **Tailwind CSS**. It follows **Atomic Design** (`atoms`, `molecules`, `organisms`, `templates`, `pages`), keeping `main.tsx` as a pure React root mount without inline HTML elements.

## Invariants

- **Product-Centric Stance**: The portal describes HelpRest strictly as a commercial technology product. Academic, institutional, or university research artifacts (e.g., MEPI, scientific initiation) must never be referenced on public pages.
- **Brand Consistency with Mobile**: The visual theme adheres to `helprest-app` styling: primary teal `#009C9D` (with variants `#007E7F` and `#21B2B3`), rating gold `#F7CC33`, dark text `#11181C`, and clean neutral backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`).
- **Atomic Separation of Concerns**: Pages are assembled solely from organisms and templates; organisms from molecules and atoms. The application entry point `main.tsx` renders `<App />` via `createRoot` without raw HTML markup.
- **Client Routing Integrity**: Routing operates via the lightweight `RouterProvider` / `useRouter` supporting client history, deep links, anchor scrolling, and automatic scroll-to-top on navigation.
- **Mandatory Play Store Compliance URLs**: The routes `/privacidade`, `/termos`, `/exclusao-de-dados`, `/seguranca-dos-dados`, `/aviso-de-saude`, and `/suporte` must remain accessible and functional, as they are referenced in the Google Play Console listing and APK metadata.
- **Strict Linting & Typing**: All source files must compile without errors under `tsc -b` (`strict: true`) and pass `eslint .` without warnings or errors.

## Integration Contracts

- **App Package ID**: `com.gustavo_lg.helprest` (Android package identifier referenced in download flows, Google Play metadata, and support pages).
- **Official Support Email**: `suporte@helprest.com`.
- **DPO / Privacy Contact**: `privacidade@helprest.com`.
- **Dietary Flags Domain Alignment**: Flag IDs (`gluten_free`, `lactose_free`, `vegan`, `vegetarian`, `nut_free`, `seafood_free`, `egg_free`, `sugar_free`) and match scoring logic match `helprest-api` domain entities (`Flag.ts`, `RecommendationService.ts`) and `helprest-app` constants.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Academic Context Leakage**: Mentioning thesis, faculty, scientific initiation, or MEPI on user-facing web pages.
- **Raw HTML in `main.tsx`**: Placing headers, divs, or inline styling inside `main.tsx` instead of encapsulating them into Atomic components and templates.
- **Broken Legal Routes**: Merging all policies into a single monolithic document or using modal-only terms, which breaks external URL validation during Google Play Console app reviews.
- **Bypassing ESLint or TypeScript**: Disabling strict mode or using `any` types in page or component props.
